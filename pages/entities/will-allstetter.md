# Will Allstetter

The subject of the site. Self-described as **"an artist, writer, and engineer
between NYC and Boston"** (`index.html:28`).

## At a glance

- **Study & fellowship** — fellow and grad student at **MIT's Art, Culture, and
  Technology (ACT)** department; BA in Computer Science and English Literature
  from **Brown** (`index.html:32`). See [[affiliations]].
- **Current work** — as of the site's writing, working at **IBM Research** on
  their incubation team "this summer" (`index.html:36`).
- **Studio** — design and commercial work through **Absent Rook Hypermedia**
  (`index.html:40`).
- **Contact** — Instagram [@willallstetter](https://www.instagram.com/willallstetter/)
  and email will@willallstetter.com (`index.html:44`); also on
  [Are.na](https://www.are.na/will-allstetter), "but less chatty there"
  (`index.html:48`).
