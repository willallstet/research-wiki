# Sea Monsters as Medium: The Bloop Case Study

**By:** Yuchin Chen — *Do Not Research* · **Published:** 2026-07-06 ·
[read online](https://donotresearch.substack.com/p/sea-monsters-as-medium)

A reading tracked for [[media-infrastructure]] and [[archives-and-wikipedia]].

## What it argues

The essay takes the 1997 NOAA "Bloop" — an underwater sound caught on military
hydrophones, later attributed to an Antarctic icequake — and argues its scientific
debunking is beside the point. Its real life is as a **medium**: it propagated via
Reddit fiction, viral CGI videos, and algorithmic amplification into "a digital
mythology of the Anthropocene." Drawing on Jussi Parikka's media ecology, Chen
links the physical internet infrastructure that carries the myth (submarine cables,
data centers, hydrophones) back to the very climate processes that produced the
sound. The claim: undefined creatures matter most as projections revealing
intertwined cultural, ecological, and technological anxieties.
