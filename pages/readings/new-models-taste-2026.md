# NM Content Today | Taste (2026)

**By:** New Models (Caroline Busta & Lil Internet) · **Published:** 2026-07-05 ·
[read online](https://newmodels.substack.com/p/nm-content-today-taste-2026)

A reading tracked for [[ai-and-machine-vision]].

## What it argues

Why has "taste" become a fixation in tech culture? The piece notes taste was
historically a defining human trait (embedded in *homo sapiens* — *sapere*, "to
taste / to be wise") and finds it telling that it is now treated as a scarce,
deployable **skill** rather than an assumed quality — an anxiety it reads as
symptomatic of an AI-saturated environment where machine judgment approximates
human discernment. It surveys recent commentary (Emily Segal's "Tasteslop", Kyle
Chayka, Paul Graham's 2002 essay, Will Manidis's "Against Taste") before its own
free-associative take, and recaps recent NM events including a Trevor Paglen book
launch in Berlin.
