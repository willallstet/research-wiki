# AI & machine vision

A dominant research interest of [[will-allstetter]]: how machines learn to *see*,
generate, and search images — and the rhetoric, aesthetics, and politics around
that. Evidenced across both his [[published-writing]] and [[art-projects]].

## In his writing

- **The rhetorical push behind AI image generation** @ ICP for *Document
  Journal* — "AI, learning, language, seeing is meaning" (`index.html:70`).
- The *Syntax* essay on the **algorithmic sublime** (`index.html:68`) sits at the
  intersection of AI and spirituality.

## In his projects

- **Vector search over images/archives** recurs: [[art-projects]] includes a
  local Are.na vector search (`index.html:72`) and a Tangible Media Group
  physical-library-by-vector-search prototype (`index.html:79`).
- **Low-view-count YouTube** as a subject — "YouTube frames from 12:00am to
  12:59am" (`index.html:64`) and a YouTube-collage post (`index.html:80`) — a
  recurring fascination with the machine-indexed long tail of video.
- **Adversarial / fragile ML** — the GPUHammer curse-tablet video frames a
  bit-flip attack dropping a model's accuracy from 80% to <1% (`index.html:76`),
  connecting to [[media-infrastructure]].

## Related teaching

- Assistant-taught **Imperfect Pictures** at SFPC (`index.html:81`, see
  [[affiliations]]) — "bad"/broken images as a subject, which overlaps with
  [[archives-and-wikipedia]] (his "Broken Images and the Alt Text that Remains").
